# 1.5.4

- Added feature flags for login providers (Apple, Email, Facebook, Google)
- Login/signup buttons are now dynamically shown/hidden based on database feature flags
- Default: only Apple and Email login enabled; Facebook and Google disabled
- Added reactions to Explore feed posts (long-press any post to react with emojis)
- Explore feed posts now show existing reactions as tappable pill badges
- Admins can edit and delete posts directly from the Explore feed (same permissions as chat screen)
- Explore feed now shows up to 3 latest posts per followed channel, followed by posts from non-followed channels
- Explore feed posts older than 90 days are no longer shown
