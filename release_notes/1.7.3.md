# 1.7.3

- Feature: Twin Falls Animal Shelter's adoptable animals now appear in Market › Pets, mixed with community listings newest first (an animal counts as listed on the day it came into the shelter); adopters message the shelter in-app
- Feature: new "Promoted" carousel at the top of every Market category, showing listings a superadmin has promoted; promoted listings also stay in the normal list with a "Promoted" tag
- Feature: superadmins can promote any listing for 7 or 30 days, or stop promoting it, from the listing's detail screen
- Feature: tapping a photo on a Market item, shelter animal or car detail screen opens it full-screen, with pinch-to-zoom and swiping between photos (same viewer as Home › Posts)
- Feature: in Market › Pets, a new button next to "Add listing" shows each photo whole instead of cropped, over a blurred, enlarged copy of the picture; tap it again to go back
- Feature: chats about a listing (cars and Market items) now show date separators (Today, Yesterday, weekday, date) between messages, like Direct Messages
- Change: pet photos cropped to fit keep the top of tall pictures (the animal's face) instead of the middle; other categories stay centered
- Change: a sold listing now disappears from the Cars, Marketplace and Pets lists 24 hours after it is marked sold; it stays visible as "Sold" until then, and in My Listings
- Change: listing cards in Cars, Marketplace and Pets now show the photo on the left and the text on the right instead of photo above text; Marketplace and Pets photos are square, and car photos run the full height of the card
- Change: the app now opens on the Market tab instead of Home
- Change: the Channels list is sorted alphabetically instead of by number of followers

# Detailed Changes

### Database (`migrations/088_shelter_animal_listings.sql`, `migrations/089_promoted_market_items.sql`, `migrations/090_market_item_sold_expiry.sql`)

- Apply 088, 089, then 090, before shipping this build: the app queries columns these migrations add (`090` adds `market_items.sold_at`, and the Marketplace/Pets lists filter on it). The Cars list also needs `085` (`vehicle_listings.sold_at`)
- The QA project (`MVC_QA`) now uses native Supabase migrations: `scripts/export_supabase_migrations.py` converts `migrations/NNN_*.sql` into `supabase/migrations/2025010100NNNN_*.sql` (so 085 → `...000125`, 088 → `...000128`, 089 → `...000129`, 090 → `...000130`)
- 088: `market_items` gains `source` (default `community`) and `external_id`, and `created_by` becomes nullable, so feed animals can have shadow rows like dealer cars. Shadow rows are visible only to the conversation's participants (`can_view_sourced_market_item`), so installed builds, which expect `created_by`, never see them
- 088: `dealer_sources.listing_type` (`vehicle` / `market_item`) plus a seeded `petango` row; the Cars source registry now reads only `vehicle` rows
- 088: `create_or_get_market_item_inquiry` routes organization-owned items to the organization; new `create_or_get_sourced_item_inquiry` materializes the shadow row on first contact and opens the conversation
- Messaging the shelter fails with "This organization is not set up to receive messages yet" until its organization is linked:

  ```sql
  UPDATE dealer_sources SET organization_id = '<organizations.id>' WHERE source = 'petango';
  ```

- 090: `market_items.sold_at`, set by the `market_items_sold_at` trigger when `is_sold` flips to true and cleared when it flips back; mirrors 085 for `vehicle_listings`. Existing sold rows are backfilled with `updated_at`
- 089: `market_items.promoted_until` (`timestamptz`, partial index). The `guard_market_item_promotion` trigger rejects changes to it unless the user is a superadmin; statements with no signed-in user (SQL editor, service role) pass, so a listing can also be promoted by hand:

  ```sql
  UPDATE market_items SET promoted_until = now() + interval '7 days' WHERE id = '<item uuid>';
  ```

### Shelter feed (`lib/modules/market/pets/`)

- `PetangoApiService` reads the PetPoint/Petango `AdoptableSearch` and `AdoptableDetails` endpoints (XML); key in `PETANGO_KEY` in `.env_qa` / `.env_prod`. The search has no date, so details are fetched for every animal (6 at a time) to get `LastIntakeDate`; everything is cached for 10 minutes
- `PetangoAnimalMapper` normalizes the feed: catalogue-style breeds ("Terrier, Pit Bull" → "Pit Bull Terrier"), `AgeGroup` instead of the mostly-zero `Age`, a `Price` of 0.00 treated as not published, HTML stripped from descriptions
- `ShelterSource` reads the shelter's name and on/off switch from `dealer_sources`; with the row inactive or no key configured, no animals show
- `ShelterAnimalCard` (list) and `ShelterAnimalDetailScreen` (photos, details, "Good to know", "Message Shelter"); an animal that is no longer listed shows "likely adopted"
- `mergePetsList` interleaves animals and community items by date. An animal older than every loaded page waits until scrolling reaches its date, so loading more never inserts rows above what was already seen
- New dependency: `xml: ^6.5.0`

### Promoted listings (`lib/modules/market/`)

- `MarketItem` gains `promotedUntil` and `isPromoted`; `createdBy` is now nullable
- `MarketRepository`: `fetchItems` filters to `source = 'community'`; new `fetchPromoted`, `setPromotion` and `isSuperadmin`
- New `PromotedListingsStrip` in `widgets/promoted_listings_strip.dart`, shown at the top of `MarketCategoryScreen` when something is promoted and hidden while searching
- `MarketItemDetailScreen`: promote menu in the app bar for superadmins only

### Photo viewer (`market_item_detail_screen.dart`, `pets/shelter_animal_detail_screen.dart`, `lib/modules/cars/vehicle_detail_screen.dart`)

- The header photos are tappable and open `ImageCarouselScreen` on the tapped photo

### Photo cropping (`lib/modules/cars/vehicle_image.dart`)

- `VehicleImage` has an `alignment` parameter, defaulting to `Alignment.center`. Pet photos pass `Alignment.topCenter` so a tall picture keeps its top: shelter animal card and detail screen, and Market items in the Pets category (list, detail, My Listings, Promoted strip)

### Whole-photo toggle (`lib/modules/market/widgets/fit_toggle_photo.dart`, `market_category_screen.dart`)

- New `FitTogglePhoto`: switches between `BoxFit.cover` and `BoxFit.contain` with a 280 ms cross-fade (`BoxFit` itself cannot be animated). Zoomed out, it paints a blurred (sigma 22), 1.6x enlarged, 55% opaque copy of the same photo behind it, on a light grey base; the copy decodes at 160 px and is built only while zoomed out. Tunable via `_backdropScale`, `_backdropBlur`, `_backdropOpacity`
- `MarketItemCard` and `ShelterAnimalCard` take `showWholePhoto`; `MarketCategoryScreen` holds the state in `_showWholePhotos` (Pets only, not persisted)

### Listing cards (`lib/modules/market/widgets/listing_row_card.dart`)

- New `ListingRowCard`: a 120 px (`kListingThumbSize`) thumbnail on the left and the text on the right. By default the photo is a square pinned to the top, and longer text makes the card taller. With `fillHeight: true` the photo stretches to the card's full height; a `Stack` with a `Positioned` photo is used instead of `IntrinsicHeight`, which network images don't support
- Also new: `SoldThumbStamp` (a smaller "SOLD" stamp for the thumbnail) and `ListingLocationLine`
- `MarketItemCard`, `ShelterAnimalCard` and `_VehicleCard` (`vehicles_screen.dart`) use it; only `_VehicleCard` sets `fillHeight`. Tags and badges wrap instead of overflowing, location moved to the bottom line, prices scale down to fit, titles are 15 pt, and thumbnails decode at 400 px instead of 800

### Sold listing expiry (`community_vehicle_provider.dart`, `market_repository.dart`)

- `fetchItems` (Marketplace and community Pets) and `CommunityVehicleProvider` (Cars) return a row only if `is_sold = false` or `sold_at` is within the last 24 hours; a sold row with no `sold_at` is hidden. My Listings is unchanged and the promoted strip still excludes sold items

### Chat date separators (`lib/modules/chat/widgets/chat_date_banner.dart`)

- `ChatDateBanner`, `formatChatDateLabel` and `buildChatItems` moved out of `direct_messages_screen.dart` into a shared file; `VehicleInquiryChatScreen` now uses them, so listing chats get the same date pills as Direct Messages

### Startup tab (`lib/main_scaffold.dart`)

- `_selectedTabIndex` starts at 4 (Market) instead of 0 (Channels)

### Channels (`lib/modules/channels/categories_grid_screen.dart`)

- `_AllChannelsList` sorts with `_sortOrganizationsByName` (case-insensitive, trimmed) instead of `_sortOrganizationsByPopularity`
